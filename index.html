FULL RULES

🎯 How to Win

Collect 3 complete property sets of different colours

Each colour shows how many cards are needed. Be first to complete 3 different-colour sets and you win immediately.

🃏 Setup

Dealing

Shuffle and deal 5 cards face down to each player. Place the remaining cards face down as the draw pile.

🔄 Your Turn

1

Draw 2 cards

Tap the draw pile to take 2 cards. If you have zero cards at the start of your turn, draw 5 instead.

2

Play up to 3 cards

Any combination: place property, bank a card, or play an action. Each card played counts as 1 play.

3

Discard down to 7

At end of your turn, if you have more than 7 cards, discard the excess. You choose which ones.

🏠 Bank

Wildcard Reassignment

Tap a wildcard already on your property area to select it (it glows purple). A purple Move Wildcard button appears — tap it to pick a new colour. This is FREE and does not use one of your 3 plays. You can flip it any time during your turn.

Building your bank

Money, action, AND rent cards can all be banked for their printed value. Once banked, an action/rent card permanently loses its special ability.

💡 Banking an action card takes 1 play but earns you cash permanently.

Paying debts

Pay from bank first. If not enough, hand over property cards at face value. No change given. Cannot pay from hand.

🚫 Just Say No

Cancel any action played against you

Can be used against Deal Breaker, Sly Deal, Force Deal, Debt Collector, Birthday, and Rent. Does NOT count as one of your 3 plays. Can cancel another Just Say No.

💡 Just Say No cancels the Double the Rent card specifically, not the rent card. Game pauses fully so you decide with no time pressure.

💸 Rent

Houses and Hotels

Add to any complete set except Railroad and Utility. House adds £3M, Hotel adds £4M more on top.
