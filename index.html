FULL RULES

🎯 Objective

Collect 3 complete property sets to win

Each colour needs a specific number of cards. Complete 3 different-colour sets and you win instantly.

🔄 Each Turn

1

Tap the Draw Pile — take 2 cards

You must draw before playing. If you have zero cards, draw 5 instead. You start the game with 5 cards already dealt to you.

2

Play up to 3 cards

Lay a property, bank a card (money/action/rent), or play an action. You can skip plays.

3

Discard down to 7

If you have more than 7 cards at end of turn, discard the excess. You choose which ones.

🏦 The Bank

What can be banked?

Money cards, action cards AND rent cards can all be placed in your bank for their £ value. Each counts as 1 play. Once banked, the card loses its action forever — it becomes pure cash.

💡 Rent cards banked = pure cash. Smart when you have no matching properties.

Paying debts

Pay from bank first. If not enough, hand over property cards (go to opponent's bank at face value). Cannot pay from hand. No change given if you overpay.

🏠 Properties

Wildcards

Two-colour wilds go as either of their 2 colours. The rainbow wild goes as any colour. Both worth £0 — cannot pay debts, but can be stolen. Reassigning costs 1 play.

Houses & Hotels

House (+£3M rent) on any complete set except Railroad/Utility. Hotel (+£4M) on top of a house. One of each per set.

🚫 Just Say No

Cancels any action against you

Works on Deal Breaker, Sly Deal, Force Deal, Debt Collector, Birthday, Rent. Does NOT use one of your 3 plays. Can cancel another Just Say No.

💡 The game fully PAUSES with a popup — you decide, nothing happens automatically.

👥 Players

2–5 players (one deck)

2 players: fast and strategic. 3–4 players: the sweet spot — best balance of chaos and tactics. 5 players: unpredictable, action cards fly constantly.

💡 This app supports 1–3 AI opponents (2–4 players total). Double deck (up to 10) coming later.
